Backend Engineer (Go)

Harbor Freight Analytics GmbH, Bremen (hybrid, two office days a week)

We track container shipments across European ports and sell forecasts to logistics companies. As a backend engineer you design and run the Go services behind our forecasting API, work with PostgreSQL and Kafka, and share on-call duty with four colleagues.

What you bring

  • Three or more years building backend services
  • Experience with Go or a willingness to switch from another typed language
  • Good German or English

What we offer

Thirty days of vacation, a learning budget of 1,500 euros a year, a public transport ticket and a quiet office near the main station. Our hiring process has a short call, a take-home task of about three hours and a conversation with the team.
