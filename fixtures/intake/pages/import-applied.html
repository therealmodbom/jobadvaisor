Platform Engineer (Kubernetes)

Kupferberg Software AG, Leipzig (office or remote within Germany)

Kupferberg builds invoicing software for craft businesses. As a platform engineer you run our Kubernetes clusters, maintain the deployment pipelines of nine product teams and keep the monitoring stack honest, together with a platform team of five.

Your profile

  • Several years of operating production workloads on Kubernetes
  • Terraform, GitLab CI and a feeling for sensible alerting
  • Comfortable explaining infrastructure to developers

Our offer

Flexible hours, a yearly conference budget, a bike leasing plan and an annual team week in the Harz mountains. Applications are answered within one week, followed by two interviews and a short pairing session on a real incident.
