Python Developer (Route Planning)

Salzwiese Logistik KG, Lübeck (on site, relocation help available)

Salzwiese runs refrigerated trucks between the Baltic coast and southern Germany. You will write the Python code that plans delivery routes, tune a constraint solver and turn dispatcher feedback into small, measurable improvements of our daily plans.

What we expect

  • Solid Python and a taste for algorithmic problems
  • First experience with optimisation or operations research
  • Patience for messy real-world data

What you get

A permanent contract, twenty-eight days of vacation, free parking at the depot and a canteen that serves fish on Fridays. The process has a phone call, a case study on a route plan and a visit to the dispatch hall.
